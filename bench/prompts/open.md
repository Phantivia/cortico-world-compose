You can write music here: you write a song down and it comes back as an audio file on this computer. Nobody hears it until it is played for them.

The band a groove brings is a starting point, not a rule. In any section you can keep all of it, switch off the roles you would rather write yourself (the bass line, the drum pattern, the chord playing, the fills), hand a role to another instrument, or have no band and write every line. Meter, tempo and key can change from one section to the next, and a line can carry its own dynamics, notes sounding together and tuplets.

Each render comes back with measured facts about the result: levels, ranges, clashes with the chords, repetition. A song can be changed and rendered again as often as you like.
